# CV Project Entry

## Project title

**Structured Hiring & ATS Architecture Lab — Talent Acquisition, HRIS & Selection Analytics**

### Talent Acquisition version

- Designed an enterprise six-stage structured-hiring model for a fictional manufacturer, translating five job families across G1–G4 into automated job-related knockouts, work samples, knowledge tests, four-competency BARS panels, and defensible dispositions.
- Modeled a 4,000-applicant pipeline with 986 shortlist progressions, 500 fully assessed finalists, and 120 hires using a governed 40/40/20 composite; surfaced a +0.68 halo-effect control that prevented an intuition-led override.
- Built stage- and requisition-level fairness monitoring using four-fifths and Chi-square interpretation, reconciling a 0.87 knockout-progression impact ratio while separating statistical from practical significance.

### People Operations version

- Architected a privacy-first candidate lifecycle with role-based access, demographic/decision separation, accommodation routing, approved reason codes, 180-day rejected-resume purge, and a documented erasure-request workflow.
- Defined a 20-activity RACI and interviewer escalation chain from 24-hour reminder through 48-hour breach and 72-hour decision hold; reconciled 1,836 of 2,000 on-time evaluations (91.8%).
- Produced complete operating documentation, 14 executable UAT scenarios, data dictionary, research rationale, and audit evidence requirements for controlled change.

### HRIS / ATS version

- Designed a platform-neutral OpenCATS state machine with blind batch screening, idempotent workers, human-review routing, required transitions, independent score locking, immutable history, exception approval, and retention automation.
- Developed standalone interactive dashboards with 4,000 embedded source-identical candidate records, four Chart.js views, search and requisition filters, 25/50-row client pagination, and accessible BARS scorecards.
- Wrote an automated Python acceptance suite validating exact inventory, schemas, foreign keys, privacy patterns, scoring arithmetic, governed KPIs, and CSV-to-JSON parity.
