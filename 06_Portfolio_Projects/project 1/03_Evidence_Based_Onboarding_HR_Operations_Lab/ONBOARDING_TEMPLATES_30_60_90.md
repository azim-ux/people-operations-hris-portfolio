# Onboarding Templates: Preboarding Through Day 90

## 1. Template operating rules

The master template applies to all APD joiners, with department, grade, location, and regulatory variants. Each activity must have an owner, due offset, status, evidence reference, and escalation route. “Complete” means the stated acceptance evidence exists; clicking a checkbox without evidence is insufficient for controlled activities.

The four evidence pillars are coded as:

- **RC — Role Clarity**
- **TM — Task Mastery**
- **SA — Social Acceptance**
- **CO — Cultural / Organizational Understanding**

Pulse scores use a 1–5 anchored scale: 1 = materially unclear/unsupported, 2 = significant gaps, 3 = workable with support, 4 = clear/capable/connected, and 5 = consistently clear/capable/connected and able to explain the context. Scores initiate conversation; they do not make employment decisions.

### Phase 0 candidate engagement cadence

These touchpoints reduce avoidable uncertainty between offer acceptance and joining. They use approved business channels, provide an opt-out from nonessential social contact, and record delivery status without copying private conversation content into the onboarding task.

| Timing | Candidate touchpoint | Owner | Minimum content and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-10 Days | Buddy welcome message and informal introduction | Buddy | Approved welcome note, role-neutral contact route, and delivery timestamp; no performance questions |
| T-5 Days | IT courier dispatch tracking number shared with candidate | IT | Courier name, dispatch reference, tracking route, delivery support contact, and delivery-risk flag |
| T-2 Days | “What to Expect on Day 1” briefing note | HR Operations | Confirmed dress code, arrival time, reporting contact, parking/security badge instructions, accessibility contact, and acknowledgment |

If equipment is not being couriered, the T-5 Days touchpoint confirms the collection location and responsible contact. Delivery failure moves into the tiered protocol in [RACI_MATRIX.md](RACI_MATRIX.md); the candidate is not treated as the cause of a vendor or internal delay.

## 6. Day 30 review template

### Required inputs

- Newcomer self-assessment against the anchored 1–5 scale
- Manager evidence from agreed outcomes and work samples
- Open access, learning, workload, or relationship blockers
- Buddy support signal limited to onboarding support—not performance evaluation

### Review questions

1. **Role Clarity:** Can the newcomer explain expected outcomes, quality standards, decision rights, key dependencies, and escalation routes?
2. **Task Mastery:** Which recurring tasks can be completed with supervision, and which require more practice or access?
3. **Social Acceptance:** Does the newcomer know who to approach, receive respectful responses, and feel able to raise questions?
4. **Organizational Understanding:** Can the newcomer describe how priorities, customer needs, values, and governance affect the role?

### Decision rules

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| Role clarity ≥4.0 and no critical ambiguity | Complete the Day 30 role-clarity sign-off |
| Any pillar 3.0–3.9 | Record one or more specific support actions, owner, due date, and evidence |
| Any pillar <3.0 or a safety/access/payroll blocker | Escalate to HR Operations and the accountable manager within one business day |
| Absence or accommodation affects timing | Adjust the plan through an individual, confidential, non-punitive review |

The analytics file records the elapsed hours to `Day 30 Role Clarity Sign-off`. APD’s target is an average below 30 calendar days; individual extensions remain possible.

### Hiring Manager Day-30 conversation guide — one page

Use this guide to cover OKR clarity, resource roadblocks, workload calibration, and buddy feedback in one structured conversation.

**Meeting record:** Employee ID: ______ · Date: ______ · Manager: ______ · Role-charter version: ______ · Next review: ______

**Before the conversation:** Review the agreed 30/60/90 outcomes, recent work evidence, open access or learning tasks, scheduled workload, and the newcomer’s optional self-assessment. Do not ask the buddy for a performance judgment or disclose confidential buddy feedback.

| Conversation block | Manager prompts | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| OKR clarity | Which objectives and key results are clear? Which priority, quality standard, decision right, or dependency remains ambiguous? Can the newcomer explain what “good” looks like for the next 30 days? | Confirmed or revised outcomes, success measures, and decision boundaries |
| Resource roadblocks | Are tools, data, approvals, training, workspace, or stakeholder access blocking delivery? Is the constraint process-owned or capability-related? | Named owner, due date, escalation level, and safe interim plan for each blocker |
| Workload calibration | Is the workload realistic for current access, training, and complexity? What should stop, start, continue, or be sequenced differently? | Agreed priorities, capacity adjustment, and review date |
| Buddy feedback | Is buddy support useful, appropriately bounded, and psychologically safe? Does the newcomer want the cadence changed or a confidential buddy reassignment? | Continue, adjust cadence, or route a private reassignment request; no performance note |

**Close:** Summarize what the manager heard, ask the newcomer to correct misunderstandings, record no more personal detail than necessary, and agree each action’s owner and due date. A score initiates support; it cannot make an automated probation or employment decision.

## 7. Day 60 review template

### Task-mastery gate

- Review two role-relevant work outputs against documented quality criteria.
- Identify tasks that can now be delivered independently, tasks requiring review, and tasks that remain restricted.
- Confirm that access matches current duties and remove unused temporary permissions.
- Record coaching, learning, or workload actions without subjective personality labels.

### Social-acceptance and support gate

- Ask whether the newcomer can obtain timely help and challenge decisions respectfully.
- Review the stakeholder map for missing or overloaded relationships.
- Confirm buddy contact remains useful and voluntary; reassign where necessary.
- Record the 1–5 social-acceptance score and a short operational action, not sensitive narrative.

### Escalation conditions

Immediate escalation applies to harassment, retaliation, unsafe work, unauthorized access, or material payroll/identity risk. Routine learning gaps receive an agreed practice plan and review date.

## 8. Day 90 review template

| Section | Evidence question | Decision output |
|---|---|---|
| Role Clarity | Are outcomes, standards, decision rights, dependencies, and escalation routes understood? | Confirm role charter or revise it |
| Task Mastery | What can be delivered independently and what controls remain? | Development plan and control boundary |
| Social Acceptance | Is the support network effective and psychologically safe? | Sustain, broaden, or repair support network |
| Organizational Understanding | Can the newcomer navigate priorities, governance, values, and cross-functional trade-offs? | Continuing context-development action |
| Overall Adjustment | What is the evidence-based 1–5 summary and where do employee/manager views differ? | Close, extend, or escalate |

### Closure criteria

The workflow may close when mandatory compliance activities are complete, critical access is reconciled, the role charter is accepted, required work evidence exists, and no unowned critical blocker remains. Closure does not mean learning is complete; continuing development moves to normal performance and learning processes.

### Supported extension criteria

An extension must record the reason category, specific support, owner, target date, and next review. Legitimate reasons include delayed access, approved absence, role redesign, insufficient practice opportunity, manager capacity, or a material process dependency. An extension must never be a hidden disciplinary label.

## 9. Thirty-day probation remediation and support protocol

This protocol covers the synthetic extended cases `APD-2026-015`, `APD-2026-018`, and `APD-2026-020` from Day 91 through Day 120. Inclusion demonstrates workflow handling; it is not a diagnosis or adverse finding. HR must first distinguish system, manager, workload, access, accommodation, approved absence, and practice-opportunity constraints from demonstrated capability gaps.

| Support control | Cadence | Owner | Evidence and safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bi-weekly HRBP coaching | Days 91, 105, and as needed | HRBP with employee | Coaching objective, agreed action, and next date; private notes are excluded from the analytical CSV |
| Documented weekly milestone targets | Weeks 14–17 | Hiring Manager with employee | Specific role-relevant target, available resources, evidence standard, result, and support action |
| Manager capability and resource check | Weekly | HRBP | Confirmation that supervision, access, workload, and practice opportunity are sufficient |
| Final Day 120 re-evaluation committee review | Day 120 | Hiring Manager, HRBP, and Head of HR Operations | Evidence pack, employee response, control exceptions, and a human-reviewed decision with rationale |

The Day 120 committee may close onboarding, continue a documented development plan under normal performance processes, or route a policy-based decision to the authorized authority. No pulse score, buddy comment, or automated rule decides the outcome. Approved accommodations and sensitive case facts remain in restricted channels.

## 10. Confidential feedback and buddy reassignment

A newcomer may report an onboarding blocker or request a confidential buddy reassignment through the restricted HR self-service case type **Onboarding Support — Private**, a private meeting with the HRBP, or the fictional monitored address `peoplecare@example.com`. The general task receives only a case reference and operational status.

- The employee may request a buddy change without giving the manager or current buddy a personal reason.
- HR acknowledges the confidential request within one business day, checks immediate safety or retaliation risk, and targets a replacement buddy within three business days.
- The HRBP limits disclosure to what is necessary to resolve the blocker; the buddy, manager, and employee cannot view restricted case notes by default.
- Retaliation, harassment, safety, payroll, identity, privacy, or unauthorized-access concerns bypass routine onboarding escalation and follow the protected incident route.
- HR records the employee’s preferred communication method, confirms the next contact, and closes the request only after the employee receives the outcome or next step.

## 12. Manager checklist

- Prepare outcomes and real work before the start date.
- Protect time for Day 1, Week 1, and 30/60/90 reviews.
- Make success standards and decision boundaries explicit.
- Provide timely, behavior-based feedback tied to agreed evidence.
- Distinguish a system/process blocker from an employee capability gap.
- Maintain least-privilege access and report access changes promptly.
- Use the buddy for navigation support, never delegated performance management.
- Escalate safety, conduct, privacy, payroll, or identity risks immediately.

## 13. Buddy checklist

- Contact the newcomer before or on Day 1 using approved business channels.
- Explain team routines and useful contacts; do not interpret policy beyond your authority.
- Schedule short check-ins during Week 1, then weekly through Day 30 and as useful through Day 90.
- Invite questions and normalize clarification.
- Do not collect sensitive personal data or create private performance notes.
- Route concerns to the correct owner with the newcomer’s knowledge unless safety or law requires immediate escalation.
- Tell the manager/HR if you cannot continue so a replacement can be assigned.

## 14. IT provisioning checklist

- Validate approved request, employee ID, start date, manager, and application/data owner approvals.
- Stage device and base account; enforce MFA and security baseline.
- Assign role-based groups, not copied access from another employee.
- Test essential access without viewing business content unnecessarily.
- Record system, group, approver, provision time, and evidence reference.
- Keep credentials out of HR task notes and email attachments.
- Reconcile temporary/elevated access at Day 30 and Day 60.
- Revoke or amend access promptly for withdrawal, move, or separation.
