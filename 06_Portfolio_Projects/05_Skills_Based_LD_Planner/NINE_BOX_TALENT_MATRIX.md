# Nine-Box Talent Matrix

## Purpose and boundary

The matrix is a structured conversation aid that combines current performance and forward-looking potential. It is not a psychometric test, succession decision, promotion guarantee, or permanent employee label. Every placement requires evidence, calibration, and an employee-accessible review route.

## Reproducible rule

Performance and potential each use a governed three-point input: 1 = Low, 2 = Moderate, 3 = High. The category is a direct lookup, not an opaque model.

| Potential ↓ / Performance → | Low (1) | Moderate (2) | High (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| High (3) | Emerging Potential — 4 | High Potential — 8 | Star Talent — 14 |
| Moderate (2) | Development Focus — 6 | Core Contributor — 16 | High Performer — 8 |
| Low (1) | Priority Support — 4 | Effective Contributor — 5 | Trusted Professional — 5 |

Total population: 70. The Star Talent cohort contains exactly 14 synthetic employees.

## Evidence standards

**Performance** should use recent, job-relevant outcomes normalized for opportunity and constraints. **Potential** should be tied to a stated future role horizon and observable learning agility, scope handling, and role readiness—not similarity to current leaders. Both inputs should cite evidence and the relevant period.

## Calibration protocol

1. Facilitator confirms definitions, time window, and role horizon.
2. Managers submit provisional inputs and evidence before the meeting.
3. Reviewers challenge opportunity bias, visibility bias, recency, halo, and affinity effects.
4. The panel compares like roles and documents any change with a reason code.
5. Data steward checks completeness and distribution shifts.
6. Employees receive development-focused outcomes, not an unexplained box label.
7. Challenges are reviewed by a person who was not the sole original decision-maker.

## Development responses

| Category | Development emphasis | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| Star Talent | Enterprise stretch, succession exposure, peer coaching | Do not assume readiness for every role |
| High Potential | Broader scope and performance enablers | Diagnose opportunity before judgement |
| Emerging Potential | Role fundamentals plus structured stretch | Do not accelerate without support |
| High Performer | Recognition, expert pathway, selective breadth | Do not force management track |
| Core Contributor | Deepen role mastery and adjacent capability | Avoid treating the center as mediocre |
| Development Focus | Clear standards, practice, and resource diagnosis | Separate capability from conduct |
| Trusted Professional | Knowledge transfer and sustainable contribution | Check whether potential horizon fits aspirations |
| Effective Contributor | Stable contribution with chosen development | Respect career preference |
| Priority Support | Immediate role clarity and support plan | Use due process; do not automate adverse action |

## Monitoring

Track placement changes, manager-level distributions, challenge rates, access to stretch work, and development completion. If protected attributes are lawfully available for fairness review, keep them segregated from routine manager views and analyze outcomes with adequate sample-size protections.
