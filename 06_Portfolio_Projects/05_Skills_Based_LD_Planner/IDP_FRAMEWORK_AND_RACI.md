# IDP Framework and RACI

## Design principles

An individual development plan connects a verified skill need to practice at work. It is co-authored, time-bounded, observable, and revisable. The employee retains voice in the goal; the manager creates opportunity; the mentor supports practice; L&D governs quality; skill owners protect the standard; and the data steward protects integrity.

## Minimum viable IDP

1. **Need:** one target competency and the evidence behind the gap.
2. **Outcome:** an observable 90-day behavior linked to the proficiency scale.
3. **Action mix:** experience, exposure, and education matched to the need.
4. **Milestones:** evidence at 30, 60, and 90 days.
5. **Support:** named manager, mentor, time allocation, and access needs.
6. **Evaluation:** pre-agreed L1–L4 evidence and owners.
7. **Closure:** employee review, proficiency reassessment, and next decision.

## 30/60/90 rhythm

| Point | Purpose | Required evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 30 | Contract and baseline | Baseline assessment, learning goal, calendar, access check | Continue, redesign, or pause |
| Day 60 | Supported application | Work artifact and mentor feedback against behavior anchors | Increase challenge or remediate |
| Day 90 | Reassess and transfer | New assessment plus manager observation | Close, extend, or set next target |

## Status rules

- **Active:** the plan is approved and at least one milestone remains open.
- **Completed:** the 90-day review and evidence decision are recorded.
- A production workflow should also distinguish Draft, Awaiting approval, Paused, and Cancelled; these are omitted from the compact synthetic dataset.
- Completion means the plan workflow closed; it does not automatically mean target proficiency was achieved.

## Quality gates

- No plan moves to Active without a target competency, baseline evidence, employee acknowledgement, manager approval, and scheduled time.
- Mentors cannot be the same record as the plan owner in this dataset.
- Day-90 closure requires evidence status and an explicit next decision.
- Bulk changes require data-steward reconciliation before publication.
- A challenged score is frozen for downstream talent decisions until review closes.

## Manager conversation guide

Start with the work outcome, ask the employee what support would make practice feasible, separate capability evidence from identity, agree how feedback will be delivered, and document only job-relevant observations. Avoid promising promotion or using the nine-box label as a verdict.
