# Skills-Based L&D Planner & Talent Growth Engine

A privacy-safe portfolio lab that turns a governed competency ontology into visible development priorities, individual development plans (IDPs), and four-level learning evidence. It models a 70-person precision-manufacturing workforce using synthetic records only.

## Decision brief

| Governed measure | Result | Decision use |
|---|---:|---|
| Workforce mastery | 81.4% | Baseline for capability planning |
| Active IDPs | 58 of 70 | Capacity and manager-coaching load |
| Star Talent cohort | 14 | Succession and stretch-assignment review |
| Mean proficiency gap | -0.48 | Difference between current 3.64 and target 4.12 |
| Level 2 learning | 88.6% | Knowledge/skill evidence after learning |
| Level 4 outcome | +24.6% yield; -18.2% scrap; ₹4.8 lakhs | Governed cohort association, not individual causation |

## What this demonstrates

- Skills ontology design with O*NET 31.0 traceability and local validation.
- Reproducible nine-box segmentation with transparent performance/potential rules.
- IDP operating design: 30/60/90-day milestones, mentor assignment, RACI, approval gates, and LMS hand-offs.
- Kirkpatrick-aligned measurement from reaction through business results.
- Source-reconciled static analytics: the CSV records and embedded browser data are identical.
- Privacy by design: pseudonymous employee IDs, synthetic names, least privilege, purpose limitation, and aggregate outcomes.

## Quality contract

The project is accepted only when all eight automated controls pass: exact inventory, schema and referential integrity, KPI reconciliation, nine-box reproducibility, privacy linting, documentation traceability, embedded-data identity, and slide behavior. No production employee data, diagnostic claims, or causal claims are present.

## Responsible interpretation

This is a synthetic decision-support demonstration. Mastery percentages are constructed signals, nine-box placement is a review prompt rather than a label of human worth, and the Level 4 figures are an illustrative cohort association. A production implementation would require local validation, accessibility review, employee consultation, bias monitoring, data-protection review, and an auditable appeal process.
